# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
A simple interest calculator that calculates the interest earned or payable on a principal amount over a specified period at a given annual interest rate.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:
- **Principal:** The initial amount of money.
- **Rate:** The annual interest rate in percentage.
- **Time:** The duration in years.

## Example
Principal = 1000  
Rate = 5%  
Time = 2 years  

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

## Result
The simple interest is 100, and the total amount is 1100.
